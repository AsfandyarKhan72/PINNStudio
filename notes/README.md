# Development notes

Records of investigations behind the v1.6.0 release; not user documentation and not shipped in the package.

- `pyside6-migration/`: the move from PyQt6 to PySide6. `PYSIDE6_TRIAL.md` (the investigation log),
  `PYSIDE6_READINESS.md` (the release-readiness report), `repro-and-traces/` (standalone reproducers of the two
  PySide6 defects found, valgrind and gdb traces, draft upstream reports).
- `licensing-proposal/`: MIT and Apache-2.0 options for a PySide6 release, with the LGPL notices. A proposal only:
  nothing in it is adopted until the rights holder approves.
