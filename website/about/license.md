## Third-party software

PINNStudio runs on DeepXDE, PyTorch, PyQt6 with Qt, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and pandas, which are installed as
separate packages and keep their own licenses; some of the interface's icons are derived from Lucide and Feather.
See [Dependencies and attribution](dependencies.md) and the repository's
[`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`](https://github.com/AsfandyarKhan72/PINNStudio/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md).

What you enter into PINNStudio (equations, constants, settings, geometry, data) and what you produce by running it or
a generated script (data files, figures, trained models, logs) are your own work. The repository's license files
state how the code PINNStudio writes into a generated script may be used.
